# Findings: RG / site-blocker homepage job

**Question:** How do responsible-gambling and site-blocker products present **one job** on the homepage?

**Products:** BetBlocker, Gamban, GAMSTOP, plus GamBlock (closest established software-blocker analog; BetFilter did not surface as a live comparable homepage in this pass).

**Method:** Four web searches + homepage / explainer fetches on 2026-10-29. Quotes are from live page text, not marketing decks.

**Not in scope here:** casino social rooms, auto-vault. Those are sibling research files.

---

## Industry noun split (read this first)

GambleAware draws the line the homepage copy often blurs:

> "Self-exclusion is when you ask gambling companies to prevent you from gambling with them."

> "Blocking software stops gambling websites and apps from loading on your phone, tablet or computer."

Source: [Gambling blocking and self-exclusion](https://www.gambleaware.org/tools-and-support/gambling-blocking-and-self-exclusion/)

Their four-layer stack is:

1. **Account self-exclusion** — GAMSTOP (GB-licensed online operators)
2. **Device blocking software** — BetBlocker / Gamban
3. **Bank payment block**
4. **Venue self-exclusion** — MOSES / SENSE / BISES

GAMSTOP's own explainer:

> "It's a way for you to avoid certain types of gambling for a specific period of time."
> "Gamstop Online helps you avoid gambling online using any websites or apps offered by companies licensed in Great Britain."

Source: [What is self-exclusion? – GAMSTOP](https://info.gamstop.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/7537077891602-What-is-self-exclusion)

**Rule for TiltCheck:** a browser overlay is layer-2 *adjacent* (device friction). It is **not** layer-1 legal self-exclusion. Calling it GAMSTOP or "self-exclusion" is a lie with regulatory teeth.

## 1. BetBlocker — device block, charity, anonymous

**Homepage:** [https://www.betblocker.org/](https://www.betblocker.org/)

### Homepage hook

H1: **"Block Online Gambling Websites"**

Supporting line: **"Free for Everyone, Everywhere"**

Proof pills immediately under the hook:

- Calendaring — Restrict in Advance
- No Registration Required
- 2 Minutes Setup

Secondary lede lower on the page:

> "BetBlocker – Free Tools to Help You Take Control of Your Access to Gambling"
> "BetBlocker helps people reduce, restrict or stop their access to gambling websites and apps."

Scale proof: ~1.3 million sites blocked, ~3.1k apps, ~134k active users. Setup claim: install in 2 minutes and "you'll have over 1,335,900 gambling websites blocked."

### Primary CTA

**Get the App** (nav + hero). Secondary: **Take the Tour**. Support/FAQ live in nav, not as the conversion.

App-store packaging is the same job in different nouns: "BetBlocker: Gambling Exclusion" / "Free Gambling Blocker & Self-Exclusion Tool."

Sources:

- [BetBlocker homepage](https://www.betblocker.org/)
- [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=mobile.betblocker&hl=en_GB)
- [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/betblocker-gambling-exclusion/id1437450983)

### Mix-in? Social / chat / tips / vault

**No.** Homepage stays on block, calendaring, parental controls, anonymity, and support tickets.

What they *do* add (still the same job):

- **Calendaring** — schedule when the restriction is on
- **Parental controls** — password-reversible, different legal/ethical mode than self-restriction
- **Suggest a Site** — users feed the blocklist
- Technical support form

What they refuse: ads, registration, wallet features, chat rooms, tips, vaults. FAQ: "BetBlocker is completely free to use and does not display advertisements or promote any products or services."

### Legal nouns

Homepage verb is **block / restrict / take control**. FAQ and store listings slide into **Gambling Self-Restriction** and **self-exclusion**.

Hard lock language (FAQ, not hero):

> "Once you have activated a Gambling Self-Restriction the only way to end it is to wait until your chosen Gambling Self-Restriction period has concluded."
> "For ethical reasons and the protection of problem gamblers our support team will not under any circumstances assist a user to disable an active Gambling Self-Restriction."

They **explicitly distinguish** themselves from GAMSTOP:

> "National self-exclusion schemes like Gamstop and Spelpaus ... only extend as far as gambling sites that are licensed in that country."
> "BetBlocker does not base its restriction on any individual countries licensees."
> "BetBlocker ... works hand in hand with national self-exclusion schemes"

They also claim UKGC/MGA-shaped cooling-off rules (auto-continue after 2+ months, 7-day cooling off). That is **software policy mimicking regulator self-exclusion**, not a legal scheme.

**Job in one line:** Install a free, anonymous, hard-to-remove device block.

## 2. Gamban — paid blocker + quit-journey costume

**Homepage:** [https://gamban.com/](https://gamban.com/)

### Homepage hook

Title + H1: **"Get The Support You Need to Quit Online Gambling."**

Lede:

> "Gamban not only provides award-winning gambling blocking, we have now added a series of tools to help you quit online gambling."

Feature 1 is still the job: **"Effective Blocking"** — "multi-layered approach to blocking tens of thousands of gambling websites and apps worldwide."

Feature 2 is the mix-in: **"Monitor Your Progress"** — days gamble-free, time and money saved.

Advantage list is recovery-clinic: Save Your Money / Reduce Temptation / Reduce Stress / Save Your Time / Feel Better / Improve Your Outlook.

### Primary CTA

**Register a FREE Gamban account** / **7-Day free trial**. Footer repeat: "Register With Gamban To Get Started…" then pay.

How-it-works is a four-step recovery path, not a product spec:

1. Register a FREE account
2. Install on your devices (one licence, all platforms, live support)
3. **Set Up Layered Self-Exclusion**
4. Stay Strong & Stay On Your Path

Sources:

- [Gamban homepage](https://gamban.com/)
- [Install Gamban](https://gamban.com/getapp)
- [Our Apps](https://gamban.com/apps)

### Mix-in? Social / chat / tips / vault

**No social room, no tips feed, no vault.** They do mix:

- **Progress / sobriety tracker** (money and time saved)
- **Live support** as an install crutch
- **Layered self-exclusion setup** inside the app (other schemes, not Gamban itself becoming GAMSTOP)
- Testimonial wall ("Join Thousands Of Others Who Use Gamban In Their Fight Against Addiction")

Registration is required. This is the opposite of BetBlocker's anonymity pitch.

### Legal nouns

Product is **gambling blocking**. Step 03 uses **Layered Self-Exclusion** as the name for *other* protections. Copy is addiction / quit / recovery, not "toolkit while you still play."

GambleAware files them under blocking software with a 7-day trial then subscription — not as a national self-exclusion scheme.

**Job in one line:** Pay for a cross-device block that babysits willpower while you quit.

## 3. GAMSTOP — legal account lock, not software

**Homepage:** [https://www.gamstop.co.uk/](https://www.gamstop.co.uk/)

### Homepage hook

Title: **"Control your gambling | Gamstop Online"**

H1: **"Block yourself from online gambling"**

Lede:

> "Take control of your gambling by blocking your access to accounts on gambling websites and apps - it's free, quick, and easy."

They sell **plain "block"** on the homepage and keep **self-exclusion** for the explainer / commission pages. That is deliberate. Cold visitors get a verb. Lawyers get the scheme name.

Secondary frames on the same page (still one job):

- "Take a break from gambling" — even if you "feel in control"
- Real-people stories (Jack, Dylan, Hannah)
- "Support a loved one who gambles"
- "Manage your Gamstop block" for people already in

### Primary CTA

**Register** → [https://www.gamstop.co.uk/register](https://www.gamstop.co.uk/register)

Register-page facts:

- UK residents only (GB + NI)
- Blocked from **all online gambling companies licensed in Great Britain**
- Periods: 6 months / 1 year / 5 years / 5 years with auto-renewal
- "A few minutes to sign up"; "up to 24 hours" to take effect
- **"Registering with Gamstop will not automatically return any withdrawable funds."**

UK Gambling Commission listing: GAMSTOP is the free **multi-operator national self-exclusion scheme** for online. Separate schemes exist for shops (GAMSTOP Betting Shops), casinos (SENSE), bingo (BISES).

Sources:

- [GAMSTOP homepage](https://www.gamstop.co.uk/)
- [Register](https://www.gamstop.co.uk/register)
- [Gambling blocking tools](https://www.gamstop.co.uk/support-practical)
- [GC: free multi-operator schemes](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-and-players/page/free-multi-operator-and-national-self-exclusion-schemes)
- [What is self-exclusion?](https://info.gamstop.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/7537077891602-What-is-self-exclusion)

### Mix-in? Social / chat / tips / vault

**No.** Stories and loved-one support are persuasion, not a second product. Practical-tools page points *out* to Gamban and BetBlocker as extra layers. No chat, no tips, no vault, no session PnL.

Scope honesty is the whole brand:

- Only GB-licensed online operators
- Offshore / unlicensed sites still exist (BetBlocker says this out loud; GAMSTOP implies it by naming its license perimeter)
- Funds are not auto-returned

### Legal nouns

Public hero: **block yourself / take control / take a break / Gamstop block.**

Legal/help: **self-exclusion scheme.**

They never claim to be blocking software. Their own tools page:

> "Install gambling blocking software on phones, tablets and computers to add another layer of protection. There is a range of software available, including Gamban and Betblocker, both of which are free for UK residents."

**Job in one line:** Register once; GB-licensed operators must lock you out for a fixed term.

## 4. GamBlock — paid irremovable software block

**Homepage:** [https://www.gamblock.com/](https://www.gamblock.com/)

Chosen as the similar product (classic software blocker). Newer analog OFFBET ([https://offbet.app/](https://offbet.app/)) sells the same job with a cleaner H1: "Block gambling. Take back control."

### Homepage hook

Title: **"GamBlock: Gambling Blocking Software – Block Online Gambling"**

Lead claims:

> "Helps Gamblers avoid becoming desperate gamblers"
> "Helps gamblers avoid the dangers of unrestricted gambling"
> "It blocks gambling absolutely."

Positioning is **anti-industry-funded** ("not funded by irresponsible gambling oligarchs") and **anti-bypass** (VPN, Tor, factory reset, poker-app install prevention, heuristics vs blocklists).

### Primary CTA

**Download** / buy a license (single or multi-device). FAQ-style "Can I try GamBlock Mobile before I buy?" lives below the fold. No "join community."

### Mix-in? Social / chat / tips / vault

**No.** Homepage is block strength, irremovability, family/institution use, and a support FAQ dump. Testimonials are family-rescue, not degens hanging out.

OFFBET (same category, cleaner page) also stays on block: on-device filter, PIN / lifetime lock, bypass detection. It adds coach chat only as a **lifetime-unlock** path — still the block job, not a lounge.

### Legal nouns

Almost entirely **block / blocking software / stop gambling app.** They do not costume as GAMSTOP or a statutory self-exclusion scheme. Target is "problem gamblers," families, libraries, governments.

**Job in one line:** Pay for software that makes gambling sites and apps unreachable, including after you try to rip it out.

## Cross-cut: how they keep ONE job

| Product | Hero job | Primary CTA | Social/chat/tips/vault on home? | Public noun | Legal noun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BetBlocker | Block gambling websites | Get the App | No (calendaring + parental + support only) | Block / restrict | Gambling Self-Restriction; they *compare* to GAMSTOP |
| Gamban | Quit via blocking | Start 7-day free trial / Register | No (progress tracker + live support + testimonials) | Blocking / quit | "Layered Self-Exclusion" as step 3 |
| GAMSTOP | Block yourself from online accounts | Register | No (stories + loved-one + manage-block) | Block yourself | National self-exclusion scheme |
| GamBlock | Block gambling absolutely | Download / buy | No | Blocking software | None; not a scheme |

Shared homepage mechanics:

1. **One verb in the H1.** Block / quit / take control. Not "platform." Not "ecosystem."
2. **CTA *is* the job.** Get the App. Register. Download. Never "Join Discord" as primary.
3. **Proof is coverage + lock strength**, not social proof of a scene. Site counts, device list, cannot-remove, GB-license perimeter.
4. **Kitchen-sink stays off the hero.** Extra tools (calendaring, progress, loved-one, parental) are the same job with a different user, or they sit below the fold.
5. **They tell you what they are not.** GAMSTOP is not software. BetBlocker is not GAMSTOP. Gamban is not free forever. GamBlock is not industry-funded.

What they refuse to be on the homepage: a casino, a chat, a tipster desk, a vault, a bankroll dashboard.

## What TiltCheck should steal

TiltCheck is a **degen toolkit**, not a regulator and not a quit clinic. Steal the *structure*, not the costume.

1. **One job above the fold.** These pages do not open with lounge + vault + tips + block. If the cold-visit job is brakes, the H1 is brakes. Hangout and AutoVault are other research files — they do not get hero billing on an RG-blocker-shaped page.
2. **CTA equals the job.** Their primary button installs or registers the protection. TiltCheck equivalent: install the extension / turn on the overlay. Secondary can be trust-check. Chat/Discord is tertiary or absent on desktop, same as these pages keeping support in the nav.
3. **Plain verb, then precise noun.** GAMSTOP says "block yourself" to humans and "self-exclusion" to the commission. TiltCheck should say what the overlay actually does (session friction, site warning, exit prompt) in human verbs — then use a *product* noun that cannot be confused with a statutory scheme.
4. **Scope honesty as a trust feature.** GAMSTOP: GB-licensed only, funds not auto-returned, up to 24h delay. BetBlocker: we cover unlicensed, we will not unlock you, we are not GAMSTOP. TiltCheck should say: read-only, not a legal lock, not GAMSTOP, will not freeze operator accounts, will not seize a bankroll.
5. **Layer language, not merger language.** Every serious page treats blocking software, operator self-exclusion, and bank blocks as *stacked* tools. TiltCheck can say "use this *with* GAMSTOP / BetBlocker" the way they already point at each other. Never "this *is* GAMSTOP."
6. **Keep social off the hero.** Testimonials and loved-one modules are persuasion for a quit product. They still do not ship a chat. If TiltCheck has a room, it is a different job — do not launder it into a blocker homepage.

## What TiltCheck should refuse

1. **Do not call the browser overlay GAMSTOP.** GAMSTOP is a UK statutory-adjacent multi-operator self-exclusion scheme. An overlay cannot register someone with GB-licensed operators, cannot bind a 6-month legal lock, and cannot return or freeze funds.
2. **Do not call it self-exclusion.** That noun means "you asked the gambling company (or a national scheme) to refuse you." Device friction, a cooldown prompt, a site warning, a session kill-switch — none of those are self-exclusion. BetBlocker's FAQ already stretches this; TiltCheck must not.
3. **Do not steal irreversible-lock claims.** BetBlocker / GamBlock / OFFBET sell "cannot uninstall until the term ends" / "survives factory reset." TiltCheck is a toolkit the user can close. Pretending otherwise is both a lie and a product-category jump into clinical RG software.
4. **Do not wear the quit-clinic voice as the brand.** Gamban's "fight against addiction" / "days sober" / "stay strong" is a different customer. TiltCheck's user is still a degen who wants brakes without being processed like a patient. Supportive, not sanctimonious. No cap: the overlay is friction, not salvation.
5. **Do not costume as a regulator or charity scheme.** No "national," no "exclusion period," no cooling-off copy copied from UKGC/MGA rules unless TiltCheck actually implements a user-chosen lock *and* names it as optional software policy, not law.
6. **Do not kitchen-sink the homepage to ape "we added tools."** Gamban already shows the failure mode: blocker hero, then progress tracker, then layered self-exclusion, then recovery pep talk. That is how a single job becomes a journey brochure. Vault, tips, and lounge do not get to ride the blocker headline.
7. **Do not imply operator coverage you do not have.** GAMSTOP's power is the license condition. Software blockers' power is the blocklist. TiltCheck's power is the tab the user already opened. Say that. Do not imply offshore sites, Stake, or a .com book are "excluded."

---

## Bottom line for homepage-job synthesis

RG blockers win by being boringly single-minded: **stop the site from being usable (or the account from being open).** They earn trust by naming the perimeter and refusing a second product on the hero.

TiltCheck can steal the single-job discipline and the noun hygiene. It must refuse the regulator costume. If the homepage job is brakes, say brakes. If it is a degen toolkit, the overlay is a **session friction layer**, not GAMSTOP, not self-exclusion, and not a vault/chat/tips pile-on.
