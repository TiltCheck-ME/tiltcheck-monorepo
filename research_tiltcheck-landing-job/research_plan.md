# Research plan: TiltCheck homepage job

## Main question

What should `tiltcheck.me` be for a cold visitor: brakes, hangout, or a kitchen-sink of lounge + AutoVault + tips? How do similar products (RG blockers vs casino social rooms vs auto-vault) present one job without lying?

## Subtopics

1. **rg-blockers** — How BetBlocker, Gamban, GAMSTOP, and similar landings state one job. What they refuse to mix in (chat, vault, tips). Expected: CTA hierarchy, legal nouns, “this is not a casino.”
2. **casino-social** — How Stake chat, Discord gambling servers, and operator “community” pages sell hangout. What feels like a casino trap vs a room nobody owns. Expected: hook patterns, what they hide (PNL, heaters).
3. **auto-vault** — How Stake.us / nuts.gg vault and third-party auto-vault scripts are explained. Compatible-casino limits. Expected: what “skim the win” language already exists.

## Synthesis

Pick one homepage job. Rank hangout, AutoVault, and lounge tips as hero vs supporting vs not-yet-public. No sportsbook metaphor.
