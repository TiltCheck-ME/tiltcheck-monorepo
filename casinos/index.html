Methodology

How letter grades are built.

  1. Curated baseline grade — Each operator starts from a researched letter grade in our casino directory. That maps to a numeric score — not vibes, not affiliate rank.
  2. Category split — Regulated, Sweeps, Crypto, Offshore, Grey Market, Skill Game, Scam — same grade can mean different risk shapes.
  3. Documented issues — Known violations and license basis show on live proof pages. Empty here is not a clean bill — it means this static mirror lists baseline cards only.
