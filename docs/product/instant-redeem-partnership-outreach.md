# Instant Redeem — Casino / Processor Partnership Outreach

**Verdict first:** Send a short email. Ask for a 20-minute meeting only as the CTA — do not lead with "can we hop on a call?" cold. Email gets the offer on record; meeting is for float/legal, not for explaining the idea.

**Who to email first:** payment processors / cashout rails (one contract, many brands). Direct casino BD second, after badge FOMO exists.

---

## When email vs meeting

| Situation | Do this |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cold / warm intro, first touch | **Email** with the offer + 1 link + soft for 20 min |
| They replied "interested" or asked about float/legal | **Meeting** (20 min, agenda locked) |
| Compliance / MSA / float caps | Meeting or shared doc — not another pitch email |
| No reply after 5–7 days | One follow-up email. Then pause. Do not spam. |

## Meeting agenda (20 minutes — only after interest)

1. **2 min** — Confirm who holds float (processor vs operator)
2. **5 min** — Covered domains + rails (ACH / Interac / crypto / etc.)
3. **5 min** — Fee share + soft/hard float caps
4. **5 min** — RG cooloff hours + irrevocable policy (non-negotiable)
5. **3 min** — Next step: sandbox enable OR production request + LOI

Do not demo for 15 minutes. Send sandbox keys before the call when possible.

## Do not say

- "We hold the money" / "send coins to us"
- "Guaranteed approval" / "live tomorrow"
- "Cancel anytime after Instant Redeem"
- That scam or ultra-low-trust shops can join
