## Ideal partner profile (score against this)

1. Already settles **deposits + withdrawals** for many casino/sportsbook merchants
2. Can hold **float** (licensed PSP / MSB / e-money) — TiltCheck orchestrates only
3. Rails match Instant Redeem: **Interac**, ACH, wallet, crypto payout
4. Cares about **RG / tilt reload** as product differentiation (same-rail cooloff pitch)
5. One commercial identity → many `coveredDomains[]`

**Skip for wave 1:** pure affiliate networks, unregulated middle wallets, anyone asking TiltCheck to hold player funds.
