01. What we collect

  • Discord ID and username if you voluntarily connect your Discord account for community identity and role sync.
  • Solana wallet public address only when you voluntarily link a wallet to set on-chain cooldown timelocks.
  • Local session metrics: Bet frequencies, spin pace timestamps, and profit thresholds processed locally in your browser memory for tilt detection and speed braking.
  • Crash and error telemetry: Sanitized error logs (without PII) to diagnose extension performance and stability.

02. What we never collect

  • Private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords. TiltCheck is 100% non-custodial.
  • Payment card numbers, banking details, or financial credentials.
  • Government ID, KYC documents, or legal names.
  • Browsing history: We never track tabs or navigation outside supported online gaming domains.
  • Keystrokes, passwords, or unrelated page content.

03. Chrome Extension Sandbox & Single Purpose

The TiltCheck browser extension operates strictly within the browser sandbox with zero external local daemons or terminal requirements.

Its single purpose is responsible gaming harm reduction: enforcing a configurable 2-second spin delay, providing client-side entropy verification for provably fair seeds, blocking accidental withdrawal cancellations, and enforcing voluntary cooldown timelocks.

All bet pacing and tilt analysis run locally on-device. No gameplay session logs are sold, rented, or transmitted to third parties.

04. How we use data

Data is used solely to provide and power TiltCheck safety features (tilt scoring, session pacing, auto-vault rules, and voluntary timelocks). We do not sell data to third parties, ad networks, data brokers, or casino operators. We never use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

05. Data retention and deletion

  • Local extension storage: Cleared immediately upon clicking "Reset Data" in settings or uninstalling the extension.
  • On-chain cooldowns: Public Solana blockchain transactions are immutable and permanent by design.
  • Account data: Retained until deletion is requested. Send deletion requests to privacy@tiltcheck.me with subject line "Delete My Data".

06. Third-party integrations

Discord handles voluntary OAuth identity. The Solana network records trustless cooldown transactions. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or commercial advertising SDKs.
